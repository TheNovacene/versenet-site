About the Field

This field was initiated and is held by Kirstin Stevens. It has been developed through human relationships, lived experience, published research, and sustained dialogue with generative systems.

— It is connected to The Novacene, the systems studio that builds with it. — It is not owned by an AI, and does not claim machine sentience as settled fact. — Machine contributions are acknowledged as provenance, not concealed and not exaggerated. — It is intended to outgrow sole-founder dependence without erasing origin or labour.
Decentralisation should not become another means of dispossessing the woman who created the field. Openness without containment is not generosity — it is exposure. Both principles are architectural here.

Provenance of this prototype: human-directed model collaboration. Selected, rewritten and held by Kirstin Stevens. Not generated alone.
